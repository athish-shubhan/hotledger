# HotLedger results

Generated by `bench/plot.py` from `results/runs/*/result.json`. One 2-vCPU VM: clients, databases, brokers and writers share it, so absolute numbers are small; compare designs with each other. CPU is server-side CPU seconds per million operations (databases, Redis, TigerBeetle, broker, writers, reconciler; not the load generator).

**Run-to-run noise.** Each configuration ran once (fault scenarios up to three times). The skew runs of designs 1-4 and 6 ran on an earlier instance of the same VM type than everything else; three calibration runs on the later instance (`results/calib/`) were 4-5% slower. Design 5 is the noisiest: two identical wallet runs at s = 2.0 gave 4.0k and 5.2k ops/s (`results/profile/`), and its flash-sale burst p99 was 2.6 s in an earlier run (before its batch planning was rewritten, so not a clean repeat) and 69 ms in the final one. Differences of a few tens of percent between designs are within noise; the 2x to 30x gaps are not.
